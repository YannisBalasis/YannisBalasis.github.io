Master's thesis · Manuscript under review at Applied Intelligence

Brain tumor MRI classification with explainable deep learning

A systematic, statistically grounded comparison of nine deep learning architectures for four-class brain tumor MRI (glioma, meningioma, pituitary, no tumor) on 7,023 scans, including my own TensorFlow implementation of the hybrid CNN–Transformer MedViT V2 and a new dual-branch feature-fusion extension.

Key finding

The Meningioma Paradox

Every model localizes meningiomas correctly at the pixel level, as confirmed by all four attribution methods, yet meningioma is consistently the hardest class to classify. The failure lies in the decision boundary in feature space, not in where the model looks — a distinction that only shows up when explainability is analysed systematically.

What I built

  • Custom CNNs, transfer-learning baselines (EfficientNet-B3, ResNet-50), Vision Transformers and a 1-vs-1 ensemble, trained under one protocol.
  • Non-parametric evaluation: Friedman, Nemenyi critical-difference, Bonferroni-corrected Wilcoxon and Cohen's d across all model pairs.
  • Explainability with Grad-CAM, SHAP, Integrated Gradients and Occlusion, with bootstrap confidence intervals.
  • A Streamlit decision-support prototype: consensus voting across architectures, interactive XAI and automated PDF reports.

Morphe — computational modeling of a gene-therapy circuit for obesity

Morphe is a non-invasive, miRNA-based gene therapy that silences HDAC6 in appetite-regulating AgRP neurons to restore leptin and insulin sensitivity. I led the dry lab: the mathematical models that predicted whether the genetic circuit would work before anything went to the bench.

What I modeled

  • HDAC6 knockdown kinetics — an 8-equation ODE system (split-intein transcription factor + shRNA silencing) predicting >95% mRNA and ~87% protein knockdown within 24 h.
  • AND-gate logic — deterministic circuit simulation confirming output only when both AgRP and FOXO1 inputs are present, keeping the therapy neuron-specific.
  • Inducible systems — AlphaFold3 structures of rtTA and CymR, blind docking of doxycycline and cumate, and ΔG→Kd→Hill dose-response curves to size safe induction windows.
  • Therapeutic efficacy — a 4-ODE model of palmitic-acid insulin resistance showing HDAC6 knockdown restores p-AKT and FOXO1 shuttling (~93% knockdown, FOXO1 ratio recovered from 0.064 to 0.25).
  • Target rationale — STRING network and RNA-seq differential-expression analysis placing HDAC6 on the leptin–JAK–STAT3 axis.

How it mattered

The models set the experimental timeline (24 h knockdown, 30.25 h readout), recommended inducer titration ranges for the wet lab, and provided quantitative benchmarks to validate the circuit against. The work contributed to the team's Gold medal and the Best Therapeutics Project award.
